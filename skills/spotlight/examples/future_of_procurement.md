# Specialists shaping the future of procurement
March 27, 2026 - News

Procurement is transitioning from cost control to a strategic function that shapes resilience, performance, and long-term value. At PIR, under Robert Liljeblad's leadership, this evolution is evident in both client engagements and the profession's overall development. Through the Nordic CPO of the Year Award, PIR contributes to recognizing experienced practitioners and elevating professional standards.

The Nordic CPO of the Year Award honors leaders who have substantially enhanced their organization's procurement function across people, integrity, and results. Its distinguishing feature is how impact evaluation works—not based on scale or prominence, but on actual accomplishments within specific circumstances.

"Strong procurement leadership is not about theory or frameworks. It is about what you actually make happen in your context." This perspective emphasizes building teams, acting with integrity, and delivering sustained results.

PIR participates as an award partner alongside EBG and an independent jury of experienced CPOs. This initiative reflects a fundamental shift in procurement perception and who determines excellence standards.

"Procurement has moved into the core of business performance. The best CPOs today are not only optimizing cost, but strengthening resilience, enabling sustainability, and actively shaping how their organizations operate."

The program establishes a learning platform where practitioners evaluate real cases, illustrating how procurement advances across different industries, obstacles, and organizational contexts.

"Progress in procurement does not come from one company or one idea. It comes from specialists with deep expertise, learning from each other and raising the standard together."

For PIR, this represents a natural progression of their approach—applying specialized expertise to genuine business scenarios while recognizing that strong functions develop through both strategic perspective and effective implementation.

"The strongest specialist firms do not just deliver in projects. They take responsibility for advancing their field and contributing to how the function develops over time."

As procurement responsibilities expand, such initiatives help showcase this development by recognizing leaders who advance the function across varying contexts, rather than promoting a single standardized model.
